Project overview

A multi-faction strategy game set in the final years of the Yuan dynasty. Natural-language control meets a living simulation, with two endings and three custom-faction policy paths.

DESIGN & EVIDENCE

From an intention to a strategy turn

Design-flow illustration. View the existing video or playable release for actual feedback.

  1. 01

    Express an intention

    Players describe an action in natural language instead of choosing only from fixed dialogue options.

  2. 02

    Apply the rules

    The simulation places that intention within faction relationships and balancing systems to produce action feedback.

  3. 03

    Carry the consequences

    Policy paths and two endings give decisions a longer-term direction beyond a single reply.

How to verify

Watch the existing video or open the game. Follow one natural-language instruction through its feedback, then compare the trade-offs between the three policy paths.
