Project overview

A narrative strategy game set in Ming-era China, driven by political decisions. Its design promise: every choice creates understandable, character-specific, sustained consequences.

DESIGN APPROACH

From a question to an experience

Give political decisions consequences that follow rules and remember the player’s choices.

Set in Ming-era China, the project connects characters, objects, and places. Development continues in Godot; the project entered a Tencent game-creation competition.

Build a world that can respond

Ten characters and five interaction steps organize the framework. Twenty-one people, forty-eight items, and thirty-three locations form the world behind the narrative.

Let AI perform; let rules decide

NPCAgent, MemorySystem, Timeline, and ConsistencyGuard constrain character dialogue. Branching narrative and nine ending tiers carry decisions into later play.
