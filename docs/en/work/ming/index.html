Project overview

A narrative strategy game set in Ming-era China, driven by political decisions. Its design promise: every choice creates understandable, character-specific, sustained consequences.

  • 10 characters × 5 interaction steps
  • 21 people / 48 items / 33 locations
  • 11 tutorial sequences
