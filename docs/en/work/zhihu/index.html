DESIGN APPROACH

From a question to an experience

Give open-ended conversational deduction a working set of rules and multiple outcomes.

As an archive investigator, the player examines a disappearance through evidence, character conversations, and conflicting information.

Design rules for deduction

Four mechanisms structure information manipulation: contamination comparison, echo evidence chains, AI-judge debates, and faction persuasion.

Deliver a playable system

The implementation includes eight characters, ten ending tiers, solo and 2–5-player modes, and a script-production workspace.
