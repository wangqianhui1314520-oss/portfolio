DESIGN APPROACH

From a question to an experience

Bring a story about hypersomnia to life through characters and voice.

A team-created educational visual novel. My work covered narrative, systems design, and audio. The completed project appeared in Tencent’s “Little Red Flower” game-creation showcase.

Organize the story around people

Sixteen character profiles and multiple storylines connect the educational theme to individual experiences and the narrative structure.

Build voice around characters and lines

Local IndexTTS 2.5 generated voice clips by character and sentence, providing narrative audio for the visual novel.
