DESIGN APPROACH

From a question to an experience

Make companionship continuous across everyday life, rather than a series of isolated answers.

DaFeiYu is an AI companion running on a personal computer and connected through QQ direct messages and group chats. The bridge is at v3.6.2. Memory and knowledge stay on the computer; model, voice, and media services may use external providers.

Remember before responding

Separate persona, long-term memory, and knowledge so that prior context can be inspected, deleted, and audited instead of treating every conversation as the first.

Reach out with a sense of timing

Proactive contact adapts to time, availability, and unanswered messages. Scheduled reminders connect companionship to concrete plans.

Engineer for continued operation

A modular message bridge connects image understanding, voice, and media tasks. A local dashboard, service watchdog, and session recovery provide operational feedback.
